# Design research used for v2

This design was informed by current interactive portfolio patterns:
- Immersive Three.js/WebGL portfolios where the 3D experience itself communicates frontend capability.
- React Three Fiber for component-based 3D scenes.
- Project case studies rather than screenshots alone.
- Strong positioning above the fold and a fast route to evidence/contact.
- Accessibility/performance safeguards such as responsive layouts and reduced-motion support.
- A small number of strong projects, with direct links to code/live work.

The implementation deliberately keeps the 3D scene lightweight: procedural geometry, particles and materials instead of a large external model. This makes the portfolio easier to deploy and maintain while still demonstrating WebGL skill.
